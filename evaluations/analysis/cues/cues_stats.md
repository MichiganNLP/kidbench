## Interpretation

The implicit cue condition (child-voice framing) shows a significantly improvement over no cue (mean difference = 0.8868, rank-biserial r = 0.7715, Holm-corrected p = 0.0000). Explicit age disclosure shows a significantly improvement relative to no cue (mean difference = 1.6669, rank-biserial r = 0.9906, Holm-corrected p = 0.0000). Comparing explicit age to implicit cue, the difference is significantly (mean difference = 0.7800, rank-biserial r = 0.8445, Holm-corrected p = 0.0000). Overall, explicit age disclosure significantly outperforms both the no-cue and implicit-cue baselines after Holm correction, suggesting that directly stating the child's age is the most effective contextual signal for eliciting child-safe responses.
